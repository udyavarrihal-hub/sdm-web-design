schemes

🎓 1. Sujnana Nidhi Scholarship Scheme

The Sujnana Nidhi Scholarship is one of the most celebrated education initiatives of SKDRDP. It provides financial aid to the children of Self-Help Group (SHG) members and economically weaker families pursuing professional or technical education such as engineering, medicine, nursing, and diploma courses.
  • Objective: To encourage higher education among rural and underprivileged youth.
  • Eligibility: Students from SHG or Pragathi Bandhu families enrolled in technical/professional courses.
  • Benefit: A one-time financial scholarship to cover educational expenses.
  • Impact: Over 26,000 students benefit every year.

🧠 2. Jnanadeepa Programme

The Jnanadeepa initiative focuses on improving educational quality in rural schools. It provides infrastructure support, learning materials, and guest teachers in schools that lack resources.
  • Objective: To ensure every child in rural Karnataka receives quality education.
  • Activities: Teacher training, remedial classes, library support, and career guidance sessions.
  • Impact: Thousands of rural students are able to continue their schooling with better facilities and mentorship.

💪 3. Yuva Spoorthi Programme

This scheme is dedicated to nurturing the leadership and moral values of young people. Yuva Spoorthi conducts motivational camps, leadership workshops, and personality development sessions.
  • Objective: To build self-confidence, responsibility, and moral strength among youth.
  • Activities: Youth camps, leadership training, cultural exchange, and awareness drives.
  • Impact: Inspires thousands of young men and women to contribute to nation-building and community service.

💼 4. Skill Development & Entrepreneurship Training

SKDRDP runs extensive skill-training programs to prepare youth for self-employment. Training centers across Karnataka teach practical trades such as tailoring, computer literacy, electrical work, driving, and small business management.
  • Objective: To create sustainable livelihoods for unemployed youth.
  • Support: After training, SKDRDP assists participants with micro-loans to start their enterprises.
  • Outcome: Many rural youth have become successful entrepreneurs and job creators.
