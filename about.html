ABOUT PAGE

Shri Kshetra Dharmasthala, located in Karnataka, is a renowned temple town known for its spiritual heritage and community service. Guided by Dr. D. Veerendra Heggade, the institution has expanded its role beyond religious and cultural work to include major social and developmental initiatives aimed at uplifting rural communities. To empower the younger generation, Shri Kshetra established the Dharmasthala Rural Development Project (SKDRDP) — a flagship programme that focuses on creating self-reliant youth through education, skill development, entrepreneurship, and moral values.

The vision behind these initiatives is to:

  • Build confident and capable rural youth.
  • Reduce migration by creating opportunities within villages.
  • Support higher education and professional learning.
  • Promote self-employment and leadership qualities.
Through its various youth-oriented schemes, SKDRDP has become a model for inclusive rural development, blending tradition with progress, and faith with service.
